# Retrv

> **Your way back to what matters.**

Retrv is a community-powered Lost & Found platform that helps people report, discover, and recover lost belongings through community participation.

Rather than functioning as a simple listing board, Retrv is designed as a social community space where users can post lost or found items, share information, communicate with one another, and help return belongings to their rightful owners.

## About Retrv

Retrv is built around the idea that recovering lost belongings is easier when a community works together.

Users can create Lost or Found posts containing useful information such as:

- Item name
- Description
- Photo
- Category
- Location
- Date lost or found

Other members of the community can browse these posts, provide information through comments, contact the poster privately, and help identify or recover the item.

## Community Lost & Found

Retrv allows members to participate directly in the Lost & Found process.

Users can:

- Report lost items
- Report found items
- Upload item photos
- Search and filter community posts
- Comment on Lost & Found reports
- Reply to comments through discussion threads
- Contact other members through private messages
- Share posts with others
- Mark items as resolved
- View community member profiles
- Help reunite lost belongings with their owners

## Lost & Found Feed

The main community feed contains both Lost and Found posts.

Each post can include important information such as the item's description, category, location, date, status, and photo.

Members can interact with posts by providing helpful information, commenting, sharing the post, or contacting the poster directly.

## Community Discussions

Retrv supports public discussions through comments and threaded replies.

This allows community members to share information such as:

- Possible sightings
- Additional item details
- Location information
- Identification clues
- Updates regarding the lost or found item

These discussions help keep useful information connected to the original post.

## Private Messaging

Members can privately communicate when more detailed information is needed.

Private messaging allows users to discuss:

- Item descriptions
- Possible ownership
- Exact locations
- Meet-up arrangements
- Item return details

Post-related conversations can remain connected to the Lost or Found item being discussed.

## Community Merit System

Retrv includes a community merit system that recognizes members who successfully help recover or return lost belongings.

When a post is marked as resolved, the post owner can credit the community member who helped return the item.

Members can earn achievement levels such as:

| Merits | Achievement |
| --- | --- |
| 1+ | Community Helper |
| 3+ | Good Samaritan |
| 5+ | Trusted Finder |
| 10+ | Community Hero |

These achievements help recognize members who actively contribute to the community.

## Member Profiles

Each member has a profile where other users can view their participation in the community.

Profiles may contain:

- Profile photo
- Name
- Username
- Number of posts
- Lost reports
- Found reports
- Resolved reports
- Community achievements
- Member posts

Verified achievement badges may also appear beside member names throughout the app.

## Search and Filtering

Retrv provides search and filtering tools to help users quickly find relevant reports.

Posts can be explored based on information such as:

- Lost or Found status
- Categories
- Subcategories
- Keywords
- Item details
- Location information
- Resolution status

## Notifications

Retrv can notify users about important community activity, including:

- New comments
- Replies
- Community merit awards
- Relevant post activity

## Core Community Flow

A typical Lost Item flow:

```text
User loses an item
        ↓
Creates a Lost post
        ↓
Community sees the report
        ↓
Members comment or provide information
        ↓
Someone helps locate the item
        ↓
Users communicate privately
        ↓
Item is returned
        ↓
Post is marked Resolved
        ↓
Helper may receive Community Merit
```
