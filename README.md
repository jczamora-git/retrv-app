# Retrv

> **Your way back to what matters.**

Retrv is a community-powered Lost & Found platform designed to help people reconnect with their lost belongings through posts, conversations, and community participation.

Instead of functioning as a simple lost-item listing board, Retrv works more like a community forum where users can report lost or found items, communicate with one another, share information, and help return belongings to their rightful owners.

---

## About Retrv

**Retrv** comes from the word **"retrieve"** — representing the idea of finding, recovering, and returning something that has been lost.

The platform focuses on making Lost & Found interactions feel more social, trustworthy, and accessible.

Users can post lost or found items, provide useful details, interact through comments, contact other community members, and earn recognition for helping successfully return items.

### Tagline

**Your way back to what matters.**

The tagline reflects the purpose of Retrv: helping people reconnect with belongings that may have personal, practical, or sentimental value.

---

## Community Lost & Found

Retrv is designed as a **community forum for Lost & Found**.

Rather than relying on a single administrator to manage reports, members of the community can participate directly.

Users can:

- Report a **Lost Item**
- Report a **Found Item**
- Upload item photos
- Add category, location, date, and description
- Browse Lost and Found posts
- Search and filter posts
- Comment on posts
- Reply to comments through discussion threads
- Mark items as resolved
- Contact the poster through private messages
- Share posts with others
- View member profiles
- Help other members recover their belongings

---

## Community Merit System

Retrv includes a simple achievement system that recognizes members who successfully help return lost belongings.

When an item is marked as resolved, the post owner may credit the community member who helped recover or return the item.

Verified merits unlock community badges such as:

| Merits | Achievement |
| --- | --- |
| 1+ | Community Helper |
| 3+ | Good Samaritan |
| 5+ | Trusted Finder |
| 10+ | Community Hero |

Achievement badges can appear beside member names throughout the community, helping highlight active and helpful members.

## Main Features

### Lost & Found Feed

A community feed containing both Lost and Found reports.

Each post may contain:

- Item photo
- Lost or Found status
- Item name
- Description
- Category
- Subcategory
- Location
- Date lost/found
- Helpful count
- Comment count
- Resolution status

### Search & Filtering

Users can quickly discover relevant posts using:

- Lost
- Found
- Resolved
- Categories
- Subcategories
- Search keywords
- Location and item information

### Community Discussions

Posts support public comments and threaded replies.

This allows community members to provide information such as:

- Possible sightings
- Item descriptions
- Location details
- Additional clues

### Private Messaging

Members can privately communicate when more information is needed.

Post-related discussions can remain grouped around the item being discussed while still belonging to one conversation between two users.

### User Profiles

Profiles contain:

- Profile photo
- Name
- Username
- Post statistics
- Lost reports
- Found reports
- Resolved posts
- Community achievements
- Member posts

### Image Sharing

Images can be attached to:

- Profile photos
- Lost & Found posts
- Private messages

### Notifications

Members can receive activity notifications for events such as:

- Comments
- Replies
- Community merit awards
- Relevant post activity

## Brand Identity

### Brand Name

**Retrv**

Short, modern, and derived from **Retrieve**.

The missing letters give the name a compact digital identity while keeping its meaning recognizable.

### Tagline

> **Your way back to what matters.**

### Brand Personality

Retrv should feel:

- Community-driven
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Friendly
- Modern
- Simple
- Approachable

The interface avoids feeling like an administrative tracking system.

Instead, Retrv should feel closer to a modern social community platform focused specifically on helping people recover lost belongings.

---

## Visual Direction

Retrv uses a clean and modern mobile-first interface.

The design emphasizes:

- Clear typography
- Simple navigation
- Rounded surfaces
- Minimal shadows
- Compact cards
- Community profiles
- Item imagery
- Easy-to-understand status indicators

### Status Colors

Suggested visual language:

- **Lost** — Red
- **Found** — Green
- **Resolved** — Blue
- **Primary Actions** — Blue
- **Neutral UI** — White, gray, and dark surfaces

The interface also supports:

- Light Mode
- Dark Mode
- System Theme

## Core Community Flow

```text
User loses an item
        ↓
Creates a LOST post
        ↓
Community sees the report
        ↓
Members comment / share information
        ↓
Someone finds the item
        ↓
Users communicate privately
        ↓
Item is returned
        ↓
Post is marked RESOLVED
        ↓
Helper receives Community Merit
```
