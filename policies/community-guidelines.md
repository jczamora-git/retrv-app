# Retrv Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

Retrv exists to help communities recover and return lost belongings.

Everyone using Retrv is expected to participate honestly, respectfully, and safely.

These Community Guidelines apply to:

- Lost and Found posts
- Photos
- Comments
- Replies
- User profiles
- Private messages
- Community interactions

Violating these Guidelines may result in content removal, feature restrictions, account suspension, or account termination.

## 1. Be Honest

Only create Lost or Found reports that you reasonably believe are genuine.

Do not:

- Create fake Lost posts
- Create fake Found posts
- Falsely claim another person's property
- Fabricate sightings
- Intentionally provide false locations
- Misrepresent your involvement in recovering an item
- Abuse the Community Merit system

If you are uncertain about information, clearly state that it is unconfirmed.

## 2. Respect Ownership

Finding an item does not automatically make it yours.

Before returning an item, ask the claimant for identifying information that a genuine owner is reasonably expected to know.

Examples may include:

- Unique marks
- Contents
- Device wallpaper
- Serial information
- Item characteristics not publicly shown in the post

Do not publicly reveal all identifying information because it may help someone falsely claim the item.

## 3. Protect Personal Information

Do not publicly post sensitive personal information.

Avoid sharing:

- Home addresses
- Passwords
- One-time passwords
- Bank details
- Credit or debit card information
- Government identification numbers
- School or employment credentials
- Private authentication information
- Sensitive personal documents

When communicating with another member, share only what is reasonably necessary.

## 4. Respect Other Members

Treat users respectfully.

Do not:

- Harass
- Threaten
- Bully
- Intimidate
- Stalk
- Shame
- Insult repeatedly
- Encourage others to attack a user

Disagreements about ownership or item details should remain respectful.

## 5. No Hate or Discrimination

Do not post content attacking or degrading individuals or groups based on protected or personal characteristics.

Retrv is intended to be a welcoming community.

## 6. Protect Children

Content that exploits, sexualizes, abuses, threatens, or endangers children is strictly prohibited.

Do not post sensitive information about minors that could place them at risk.

Any suspected child exploitation content may be removed immediately and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required.

## 7. No Sexual or Explicit Content

Do not upload or send sexually explicit, pornographic, or sexually exploitative content.

Retrv is a Lost and Found community, not an adult-content platform.

## 8. No Illegal Activity

Do not use Retrv to:

- Sell illegal goods
- Facilitate theft
- Traffic stolen property
- Coordinate fraud
- Threaten violence
- Extort users
- Blackmail users
- Facilitate other unlawful activity

Found belongings must not be used as leverage for unlawful demands.

## 9. No Scams

Do not attempt to deceive users seeking lost belongings.

Examples of prohibited behavior include:

- Claiming to have an item you do not possess
- Requesting payment before providing reasonable proof
- Sending fraudulent payment links
- Requesting bank credentials
- Requesting authentication codes
- Impersonating another person
- Creating fraudulent reward schemes

If something appears suspicious, report the user.

## 10. Use Private Messages Responsibly

Private messaging should primarily support Lost and Found interactions.

Do not send:

- Spam
- Repeated unwanted messages
- Threats
- Harassment
- Sexual content
- Fraudulent links
- Unrelated advertisements

If another user asks you to stop contacting them, respect that request.

Users may block accounts when necessary.

## 11. Post Relevant Content

Keep discussions connected to the Lost or Found report.

Helpful contributions include:

- Possible sightings
- Location details
- Item descriptions
- Ownership clues
- Updates about recovery

Avoid repetitive, unrelated, or intentionally disruptive comments.

## 12. Photos

Only upload images that are relevant to the Lost or Found report, profile, or conversation.

Do not upload images that:

- Violate another person's privacy
- Contain illegal content
- Are sexually explicit
- Contain threats
- Intentionally expose sensitive documents
- Infringe another person's rights

When possible, blur or hide sensitive information visible in photographs.

## 13. Safe Meetups

If you need to meet another user to return an item:

- Meet in a public location
- Prefer well-lit areas
- Consider bringing someone with you
- Inform someone you trust
- Avoid unnecessarily sharing your home address
- Verify ownership before transferring the item

Retrv does not supervise in-person meetings.

## 14. Rewards

Users may voluntarily offer rewards where lawful.

However, users must not:

- Demand unreasonable payment as a condition for returning property
- Threaten to keep or destroy property unless paid
- Misrepresent ownership to obtain a reward
- Use rewards as part of a scam

## 15. Community Merit

Community Merit exists to recognize genuine assistance.

Do not:

- Trade merits
- Sell merits
- Create fake accounts to award merits
- Award yourself
- Arrange fake Lost and Found activity to obtain badges
- Manipulate the achievement system

Fraudulent merits may be removed.

## 16. Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, use Retrv's reporting tools.

You may report:

- A post
- A comment or reply
- A user
- Other inappropriate content

When reporting, provide accurate information.

Do not abuse the reporting system to harass another user.

## 17. Blocking

You may block another user where the feature is available.

Blocking may restrict the blocked user's ability to:

- Send you private messages
- Interact directly with you
- Appear in certain parts of your experience

Blocking does not replace reporting serious misconduct.

## 18. Moderation

Retrv may review reported content and take actions including:

- Removing content
- Issuing warnings
- Restricting features
- Removing fraudulent merits
- Temporarily suspending accounts
- Permanently terminating accounts

The severity of action may depend on:

- Nature of the violation
- Risk to other users
- Repeated violations
- Previous moderation history

Severe safety violations may result in immediate action.

## 19. Emergency Situations

Retrv is not an emergency service.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger or a crime is occurring, contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement service.

Do not rely on Retrv for emergency response.

## 20. Help Keep Retrv Useful

Retrv works best when community members:

- Report honestly
- Share useful information
- Protect each other's privacy
- Return belongings responsibly
- Communicate respectfully
- Report serious misuse

Every responsible contribution helps make the community more useful.
