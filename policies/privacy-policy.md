# Retrv Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

Retrv ("Retrv", "we", "our", or "us") is a community-powered Lost & Found platform that allows users to report lost or found belongings, communicate with other community members, and help return items to their rightful owners.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Retrv collects, how that information is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

By using Retrv, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Retrv may collect information that you provide when creating an account, using the application, or interacting with other members.

### Account and Profile Information

Depending on the current version of Retrv, account and profile information may include:

- Name
- Username
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile photo
- Account or user identifier

Your username, profile photo, display name, community statistics, posts, and achievement badges may be visible to other Retrv users.

Phone numbers and email addresses are not intended to be publicly displayed unless a feature clearly informs you otherwise or you choose to share them yourself.

### Lost and Found Posts

When you create a Lost or Found post, we may process information including:

- Item name
- Item description
- Item photo
- Category and subcategory
- Location information entered by the user
- Date the item was lost or found
- Lost, Found, or Resolved status
- Post creation and update dates

Information included in public posts may be visible to other Retrv users.

Retrv does not currently require precise device GPS location for ordinary Lost and Found posts. Location descriptions that you manually enter into a post may be publicly visible.

### Comments and Replies

When you participate in public discussions, Retrv may store:

- Comment text
- Replies
- Author identifier
- Date and time of the comment
- Post associated with the comment

Comments and replies are considered public community content.

### Private Messages

Retrv may store messages exchanged between users, including:

- Message text
- Shared images
- Sender and recipient identifiers
- Conversation information
- Post or thread context
- Message timestamps

Private messages are intended to be visible only to the participants in the conversation.

Retrv may access or preserve message information where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce community rules, protect users, resolve technical issues, or comply with legal requirements.

### Uploaded Images

Users may upload images for:

- Profile photos
- Lost and Found posts
- Private messages

Uploaded images may be processed and stored through Retrv's storage or hosting providers.

### Community Activity

Retrv may process information relating to:

- Helpful reactions
- Comments and replies
- Notifications
- Resolved posts
- Community Merit awards
- Achievement badges

### Technical Information

Retrv and its service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate the application, such as:

- Device or browser information
- Application version
- Network information
- Error and diagnostic information
- Authentication or session identifiers

We do not intentionally collect information that is not reasonably necessary for operating Retrv.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Create and maintain user accounts
- Display user profiles
- Publish Lost and Found reports
- Allow community discussions
- Enable private messaging
- Upload and display images
- Search and filter Lost and Found posts
- Send in-app notifications
- Maintain Community Merit and achievement features
- Help users identify or recover lost belongings
- Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, or misuse
- Investigate reports and enforce community rules
- Maintain application security
- Diagnose technical problems
- Improve Retrv
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

Retrv does not sell users' personal information.

## 3. Public Information

Retrv is a community platform.

Information you intentionally place in public areas may be visible to other users.

This may include:

- Display name
- Username
- Profile photo
- Achievement badge
- Lost and Found posts
- Public comments and replies
- Post images
- Community statistics

Do not include sensitive information in public posts or comments.

For your safety, avoid publicly sharing information such as:

- Home address
- Passwords
- Government identification numbers
- Financial information
- Authentication codes
- Private phone numbers
- Sensitive personal documents

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

Retrv may rely on third-party technology providers to operate the application.

Depending on the current deployment, these may include services such as:

- Supabase for database, authentication, backend, or storage services
- UploadThing or another storage provider for image uploads
- Vercel for web hosting or server-side functionality
- Google Play and Android services for application distribution

These providers may process limited information necessary to provide their services.

Their handling of information is also governed by their respective privacy policies and contractual terms.

Retrv will not intentionally provide third parties with personal information for unrelated advertising or data-sale purposes.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through Retrv.

These measures may include:

- Secure HTTPS connections
- Authentication controls
- Database access rules
- Row Level Security or similar authorization rules
- Restricted access to server-side credentials
- Access controls for private information

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

## 6. Data Retention

Retrv retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Maintain your account
- Provide application functionality
- Preserve Lost and Found discussions
- Maintain community safety
- Resolve reports or disputes
- Prevent fraud or abuse
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information where reasonably possible and legally required.

Certain information may be retained temporarily where necessary for:

- Security
- Fraud prevention
- Abuse investigations
- Legal compliance
- Backup recovery

Any retained information will not be used for unrelated purposes.

## 12. Lost and Found Safety

Retrv helps community members communicate about lost and found belongings but cannot guarantee:

- That a reported item exists
- That a person claiming an item is its lawful owner
- That information posted by users is accurate
- That a user will safely return an item

Users should verify identifying details before transferring property.

Where an in-person exchange is necessary, users are encouraged to meet in a safe and public location.

Do not send money, passwords, authentication codes, financial details, or sensitive identification information to another user.
